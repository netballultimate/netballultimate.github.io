Privacy policy

Netball Ultimate is a netball game for Android, Windows and the web. This page explains what information the game uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: we don't ask for your name, email or location, and we don't sell anything about you.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress is saved on your own phone or computer, not on our servers. That includes:

  • your athletes (the names you give them, their looks, positions and stats);
  • your team name, country and kit colours;
  • coins, gems, items and training sessions;
  • settings such as volume, difficulty and your on-screen control layout.

We can't see this data. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Playing with other people

When you play someone on the same network (LAN) or join a game by address, the two devices connect directly. To do that, each device can see the other's IP address, and the game sends match information between them, such as your team name, kit and what happens on court. None of this passes through or is kept on our servers.

Online matchmaking isn't available yet. We'll update this policy before it launches.

The game engine

Netball Ultimate is made with Unity. Unity may collect basic, anonymous technical information about your device (for example the model, operating system and graphics chip) to help improve its engine. You can read how Unity handles this in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Most are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward, such as extra coins or a free training session. Sometimes a full-screen ad plays between offline matches. Ads never play during a match, and players who have bought anything don't see the between-match ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, rough location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's own use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your answer at any time with the PRIVACY button in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). On iPhone, the game asks before allowing tracking, and you can say no.

Purchases

The game doesn't currently sell anything for real money. If we add purchases, payments will be handled by the app store, and we will never see your card details.

Information we don't collect

We don't collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos. The game doesn't need an account to play. The advertising data above goes to Google, not to us.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads shown to players we know are under 13 are requested as child-directed, which means no personalised ads and no tracking. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

The website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its text font is loaded from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they may log your IP address and browser details when you visit. The site doesn't set its own cookies or use tracking scripts.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For big changes, such as online play, we'll also say so in the game's update notes.
