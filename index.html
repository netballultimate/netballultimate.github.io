Why it plays different

Netball, properly

Real rules

Three seconds to pass, footwork, offside zones, contact and obstruction. If it's a whistle on court, it's a whistle here.

Smart teammates

Defenders mark ball-side, attackers hold and make short leads. Every pass is a read, not a guess.

Skill shots

Chest, lob and low passes. Time your shot on the release meter, and time your jump to block one.

Your athletes

Create players, pick hair, kit and boots, then train their speed, agility, accuracy and jump.

Know your zone

Seven positions

Every bib has its own patch of court. Step outside it and it's offside. Pick a position to see where it can go.
